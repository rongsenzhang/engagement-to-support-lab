## Observation-to-decision table

| Task / anonymous record reference | Observable action | Participant explanation | Researcher's provisional interpretation | Contrary evidence / alternative explanation | Proposed design change | Actual change made | Evidence after revision / unresolved issue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| To be collected | To be collected | To be collected | To be analysed | To be sought | To be decided | Not yet made from user evidence | Not yet tested |

Do not merge observations with interpretations. For example, an extra click is observable; a claim that someone “distrusted the model” needs their explanation or must remain a tentative interpretation. Do not write illustrative examples into this table as if they were collected observations.

## Coding and contrary examples

Begin with clarity, missing context, probability/causality understanding, workflow fit and suggested next action. Add new categories when actual evidence requires them. Record which excerpts support a category, which do not, and where the researcher remains uncertain.

Report counts such as “two of four participants requested clarification on …” only when supported by actual records. Avoid population percentages, significance tests or claims of broad effectiveness from 3–5 convenience-sampled volunteers. Quotes require the participant's agreement and a check for identifying context; otherwise paraphrase or omit them.

## Product decision after actual sessions

1. What specific misunderstanding or barrier was observed, and in how many actual sessions?
2. What alternative explanations remain plausible?
3. What change was made, and which part of the interface demonstrates the before/after difference?
4. Was the revision tried again with consenting volunteers? State the actual number and procedure.
5. What disagreement or unresolved limitation remains?

Keep a proposed design response labelled proposed until implemented. Keep an implemented response labelled untested until the relevant follow-up occurs. A developer's own inspection may motivate a change, but must not be described as participant-driven evidence.

## Before publishing a synthesis

Confirm the agreed withdrawal window has passed, honour any withdrawals, remove identifying detail, and publish only the anonymised synthesis and actual design evidence. Record the planned deletion date for raw notes, no later than 30 days after synthesis unless a shorter period was agreed.
