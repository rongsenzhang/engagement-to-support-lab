# Participant information and agreement

**Prepared material — no sessions have yet been conducted.**

## Invitation

You are invited to a 20–25 minute session about an independent research prototype called Engagement-to-Support Lab. We are looking for 3–5 adult volunteers with experience of tutoring, student support or learning analytics. The session explores whether the interface helps people understand evidence, recognise uncertainty and identify an appropriate next check.

This is a formative interface study. It is not an assessment of your professional competence, a study of your students, or a test of whether contacting a student prevents withdrawal. The prototype is independent of Kortext and is not integrated with an institution's services.

## What you would do

You would use four short tasks involving **invented learner records only**: compare activity patterns, reconsider a missing submission after extra hypothetical context, choose five records for further review, and explain an illustrative prediction error. The cases, scores and outcomes are synthetic. No real student is assessed or contacted.

You may think aloud and explain what is clear, uncertain or unsuitable. You can ask for help, skip any task or question, pause, or stop without giving a reason. Taking part is voluntary; declining or withdrawing has no effect on your role or relationship with the project owner.

Please do not share real student records, names, personal circumstances, institutional screenshots or confidential work information. General comments about your experience are enough. If identifying details are mentioned accidentally, the facilitator will omit them from the notes.

## Notes and intended use

With your agreement, the facilitator will record short, de-identified notes about actions, approximate task times, requests for help and your explanations. No audio or video recording is planned. The record uses a code such as P1 and a broad role category, without your name or contact details.

The page keeps this record in the current tab until the facilitator explicitly downloads it. It does not upload the notes or save them automatically in browser storage. Downloaded records will be kept in an access-controlled local project location, outside the public repository. Recruitment/contact details will be held separately.

The owner intends to use an anonymised synthesis in a public project portfolio: descriptive counts, provisional themes, contrary examples and changes to the prototype. Raw session notes will not be published. The small convenience sample will not be presented as evidence of population-wide effectiveness. Any quotation proposed for publication must be checked for identifying context and agreed with the participant; otherwise paraphrase or omit it.

Raw session notes will be deleted within 30 days after synthesis, or sooner if agreed. Before the session, the facilitator must explain the planned synthesis date and agree a withdrawal deadline. Use your participant code when asking to withdraw through the separately agreed contact route. Before that deadline, identifiable session records can be removed from the analysis. Once a fully anonymised, combined synthesis has been published, it may no longer be possible to isolate an individual's contribution; explain this before agreement.

## Agreement record

The facilitator should ask the participant to confirm each statement. Do not pre-tick agreement or infer it from attendance.

- I am an adult and understand the purpose and activities described above.
- I have had an opportunity to ask questions.
- I understand that the examples are invented and that no real learner action is taken.
- I understand that participation is voluntary and that I can skip, pause or stop.
- I agree to short de-identified notes, including approximate active-task times, for the stated purpose.
- I understand the proposed public anonymised synthesis, storage/deletion arrangements and agreed withdrawal deadline.
- I agree to take part in this session.

Record agreement using the participant code and date. A name or signature is not required by this prototype; follow any applicable institutional process separately. If agreement is absent, use **Practice** for the facilitator's own rehearsal and do not count it as a participant session.
